# Headsub Team

This directory contains data and configuration files for the Headsub team in the Shortblock group of the Powertrain department.

## Team Description

The Headsub team is responsible for cylinder head sub-assembly operations. They prepare and assemble cylinder head components before they are integrated into the engine.

## Workstations

The Headsub team operates the following workstations:

1. **Parts Wash** - Cleaning and preparation of cylinder head components
2. **H010** - Initial cylinder head assembly (loading job)
3. **H080/H090** - Valve installation and adjustment
4. **H100** - Seal installation
5. **H110/H120** - Quality checks and measurements
6. **H170** - Final cylinder head assembly (loading and heavy job)
7. **BW010** - Block-to-head mating (loading and heavy job)
8. **BW070** - Torque application and verification
9. **M050** - Additional component installation (loading and heavy job)
10. **M090** - Final quality checks (loading and heavy job)

## Team Structure

The team typically consists of:
- 1 Team Leader
- 1 Backup
- Several Associates

## Scheduling Considerations

- Heavy jobs (H170, BW010, M050, M090) should be rotated among employees to prevent fatigue
- Loading jobs should be distributed evenly
- Employees should rotate through different workstations to maintain skills
